HOW TO PLAY

Pick a hero. Everyone has their own deck. Knock out everyone else to win.

YOUR TURN

  • Draw 1 card, then play 1 card.
  • Cards with ⚡ Play again let you keep going.
  • If your hand is ever empty on your turn, draw 2.
  • You get 60 seconds per play. Take too long and a random card gets played for you.

CARD SYMBOLS

SHIELDS

Shield cards stay in front of you and soak damage until they break. Damage hits your oldest shield first.

THE MAZE COLLAPSES

After every hero has had 12 turns, falling rocks hit each player at the start of their turn, and they hit harder every round. No endless games.
